05 House Rules Applies to everyone, every shift
·Keep every room clean — grow room, pesticide room, storage room, and tool room.
·Return tools to their place — don't leave them in the hallway at end of day.
·Return the pushing chart to the outside water room.
·Disconnect the water hose when you're done using it.
·Pot checking is a full inspection, not just your assigned task — if a table is uneven, flag it to the group for a full pot check.
·Don't place pots randomly while pot checking.
·Plant spacing goal: twice a week for weeks 1–3, then once a week for weeks 4–9.
·Net layers: 1st layer uses green net; 2nd–4th layer uses white net.
